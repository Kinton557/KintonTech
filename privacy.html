Privacy notice

What we collect and why, in plain words.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and we never see them.
  • Order details: what you ordered, your phone number and any note you add.
  • Messages you send through the contact form.

Why

Only to run your account, deal with your orders and reply to your messages. We do not sell your information.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase to store accounts, orders and messages.
  • Google reCAPTCHA to stop automated spam. It is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Google Analytics to count visits to the site.
  • Your cart is kept in your own browser until you place an order.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, message us on WhatsApp and we will help.

This is a plain-language starting notice. Have it reviewed if your business grows.
