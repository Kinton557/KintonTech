Questions people ask

Do I pay when I place an order?

No. Placing an order pays nothing. We contact you to agree scope, price and timeline, then your invoice appears in your account with a Pay now button.

How can I pay?

Through Paynow: EcoCash (a PIN prompt appears on your phone), InnBucks, Zimswitch and bank payments. Paynow handles your PIN and card details, so we never see them.

Can you build something that is not in the shop?

Yes. Send us a message or use the quote request. Apps and other custom work are scoped with you first, then we send a fixed quote.

How do I get help after I order?

Message us on WhatsApp, or open a support ticket from your account. Every invoice also has an Ask us on WhatsApp button.
