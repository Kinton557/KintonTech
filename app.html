Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the Install app button here, or the ⋮ menu then Install app.
  3. Tap Install. The Kinton icon appears on your home screen.

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
